OpenNotch

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 30, 2026

OpenNotch turns your voice into text and actions on your Mac. This page explains what leaves your Mac, where it goes, and why. The short version: your audio never leaves your Mac, and we don't sell or share your data for advertising.

Your voice

Speech is turned into text on your Mac, using Parakeet or Apple's on-device speech engine. Audio is never uploaded, and it isn't saved after a dictation ends.

Text we send to AI models

Some features send text (not audio) to an AI model:

  • Dictation cleanup sends the transcript, the name of the app you're typing into, and your custom words to Anthropic's Claude.
  • Commands send what you said, plus the names of your apps and the labels of buttons and fields on screen, to a decision model (Jev, by TypeSafe, through Vercel's AI Gateway). Multi-step commands can also include text visible in those fields.
  • Form filling sends the text you copied and the form's field labels to Claude.

With OpenNotch Pro, these requests pass through our server at opennotch.ai, which checks your subscription and forwards them. We don't store their contents. If you use your own API keys instead, requests go straight from your Mac to that provider under your agreement with them. You can turn cleanup off, or run it with rules only, in Settings.

Your account

When you sign in, we store your email address and the sign-in method you used (Apple, Google, GitHub or email) with our authentication provider, Supabase. We use your email to sign you in, to connect your account to your Pro subscription, and to send account and billing messages.

Payments

Pro is sold by Polar, which acts as the merchant of record. Polar handles your payment details; we never see your card number. Polar tells us your email, your subscription status and your license key.

What stays on your Mac

Your settings, custom words, usage counters and API keys stay on your Mac. Keys are kept in the macOS Keychain.

Updates and the website

The app checks opennotch.ai once a day for updates. It doesn't send any information about your Mac. Our website and server are hosted by Vercel, which keeps standard request logs such as IP addresses for a limited time.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time in Settings. To delete your account and the data tied to it, contact us and we'll do it within 30 days. Subscriptions can be cancelled from Manage subscription.

Children

OpenNotch isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update this page and tell you in the app or by email.
